# Learner profile

Shared across every course. Course-specific notes stay in each course's `NOTES.md`.
Fill this in as you learn about the learner; ask them in the first session if it's empty.

## Who they are
- (What they do, what they're working towards, what they already know.)

## How they learn best
- (Pictures or text first? Code? How much maths, and how formal? Write what the evidence shows, with a date.)
- Self-reports like "everything was clear" are not evidence; quiz results and "your turn" answers are.

## House style for lessons
- High-fidelity, professional reading interface: full HTML pages, a single column with sidenotes,
  shared `_shared/assets/course.css`, light and dark themes, works at phone width.
- Every lesson: one tangible win, a spaced warm-up from an earlier lesson, quizzes with equal-length options
  and hints on wrong answers, a "recall tomorrow" prompt, one primary source, cited links, and an
  "ask me" box. Each lesson gets a reference cheat sheet.

## Privacy
- (Anything that must never appear in a lesson, or courses that are private.)
